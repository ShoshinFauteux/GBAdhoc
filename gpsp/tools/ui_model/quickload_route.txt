# quickload_route.txt: the quick load's browser through the real menu (setup_quickload.sh):
# slots 1 and 3 hold a state, 2 is empty.  SELECT+R asks for slot 1; DOWN skips to slot 3; SQUARE
# does nothing; DOWN and X on one frame load slot 3, the slot the screen shows (EVT state_load
# file=...st2).  A step that does not happen is EVT route_fail, exit 8.
+400 route boot
+1 press SELECT+R
+1 see "Load Slot 1?" 20 no_question
+1 route asked
+12 dump ql_slot1
+2 press DOWN
+1 see "Load Slot 3?" 20 no_browse
+1 route browsed
+12 dump ql_slot3
+2 press SQUARE
+6 see "Load Slot 3?" 10 stray_key_answered
+2 press DOWN+CROSS
+1 wait menu_closed 20 no_close
+1 route loaded
+90 dump ql_after
+30 exit 0
